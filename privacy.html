Privacy policy

Effective September 2026

This policy explains how Morvesto Digital LLC ("Morvesto Digital," "we," or "us") handles information in connection with this website, morvesto.com. Each of our apps will also have its own privacy details describing exactly what that app collects and why.

Information we collect on this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or sign-up forms. We do not collect personal information through the site itself.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep a record of our correspondence.

Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to operate the service. We do not have access to use this information to identify you. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Our apps

Our apps are built to collect only the information needed to provide their features. Before you download an app, its App Store or Google Play listing and its own privacy details will describe what it collects, how it's used, and your choices.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate (such as email and hosting providers), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the company.

Children

This website and our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you've sent us by emailing privacy@morvesto.com.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here and change the effective date above.
